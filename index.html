Gridsworn Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Gridsworn (“the game”) is a turn-based strategy game made by Jack Harris under the name Automatic Writing (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The short version: Gridsworn does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Information we collect

None. Specifically, the game does not:

  • require you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your name, email address, or any contact details;
  • collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data;
  • show advertisements or include advertising or tracking software;
  • use analytics or crash-reporting services;
  • offer in-app purchases or collect payment information;
  • track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (for example, your sound preference) on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the game removes it. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, your device settings may include this game’s saved data in those backups; that is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Third parties

  • Apple. The game is distributed through the Apple App Store and TestFlight. Apple handles downloads and may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as download counts) and crash reports from users who chose to share them. We cannot use these to identify you. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Unity. The game is built with the Unity engine. We have turned off Unity’s optional analytics, advertising, and purchasing services. See Unity’s Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have a question or request about your privacy, contact us and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
